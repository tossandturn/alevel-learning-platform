## Deliberate product decisions, not hidden dismissals

- **Do not reintroduce a suspect-held evidence gate.** A reviewer continued to penalize the public-investigation path because one person can retrieve every public record. The product keeps that anti-deadlock property. It does not require two roles to countersign, force a human to speak, or invent a police substitute for their testimony. The record is inspectable; motives, admissions, private conversations, questions and final individual votes remain distinct. This tradeoff is documented instead of claiming the external critic awarded a higher score.
- **Private intentions are not performed actions.** Remove repetitive `consequence` text rather than claiming that choosing an intention physically turned paper, confessed or made another person understand. Each choice gives an immediate, different, role-specific reflective/action question. The player may say it voluntarily; no automated actor is handed a human's private choice.
- **Do not create another identity-disclosure gate in Venice.** “先聊别的，稍后再谈” concerns when the character speaks; it is not a permanent veto over the fictional archive's authorized identity page. The options now say that accurately. Five-year correspondence and the third-act relationship choices remain separate.
- **No score shopping.** Third-pass model verdicts remain `revise`. Factual inconsistencies, early leaks, wrong pronouns and missing runtime fallback were repaired; subjective requests for more compulsory mechanics were evaluated against agency and the anti-deadlock design. The final packet is labeled exported/not newly reviewed unless an additional real review is performed.
